Audit Overview & Methodology

This report contains a comprehensive Conversion Rate Optimisation (CRO) and UX audit of the official Al Hilal SFC online store (store.alhilal.com). Conducted in August 2026, the audit relies exclusively on direct, bottom-up DOM analysis, heuristic evaluation of the mobile purchasing path (Mobile First), and gap analysis against top-tier European football clubs (Real Madrid, FC Barcelona, Manchester City, Arsenal, AC Milan).

Note: No GA4 or Shopify backend data was provided prior to this audit; findings are derived from storefront code, network payloads, and UI/UX friction mapping.

09 What we didn't check & Truth Checker

What we didn't check

  • Checkout — requires a product in cart and a payment session; intentionally out of scope.
  • Search autocomplete — placeholder «Search for anything» visible, live suggestions not tested.
  • Stock status — PDP shows no inventory state; cannot be verified externally.
  • Alt attributes and WCAG contrast — not measured pixel-wise.
  • GA4/Clarity data — stack exists (GA4 + GTM) but no funnel access; A6 (friction) needs measurement.
  • Cart with a product — only the empty state was scored; ATC → drawer → checkout not run end-to-end.
  • Desktop — intentionally out of scope (mobile-only report).

Truth Checker

What I know from the data: section order measured in the DOM (px positions), counters (0 product links, 2 carousels, 6 hero slides, 0 review widgets, 34 products, 12 cards), PDP details (price above the fold, ATC 52 px at 1803 px, no amount in the button, delivery accordions), 18.08 benchmark (element_scores, priority_gaps, 17 described screenshots).

What I assume: that the behaviour visible on 19.08 (section order, no reviews) is a stable state, not campaign ephemera; that PLP card scores apply to the whole store, not only Official Kits.

What may be wrong: A2 scoring is based on DOM + benchmark, not pixel measurement (contrast, alt); priorities 0–24 use estimated impacts — without GA4 data they are literature hypotheses.

What to verify before acting: (a) GA4 event map — are add_to_cart / view_item_list collected, (b) whether a sticky ATC conflicts with Tabby/chat, (c) the review vendor decision (Judge.me vs Okendo — MENA/Shopify), (d) whether «Back to school» is a permanent hero slide.

What I would improve with one more input: GA4 funnel data (view_item → add_to_cart → begin_checkout → purchase) — then A6 enters the report and priorities stop being estimates.
