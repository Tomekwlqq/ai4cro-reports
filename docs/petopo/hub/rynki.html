Which market to start with

Underwater/water treadmill for dogs and horses; audiences: veterinary clinics, dog breeding kennels, horse stud farms. Germany is not a market. Research of 26.11, quick: “not found” and “not confirmed” do not mean “does not exist”. Conversions: NBP rate of 25.11.2026. Petopo's price in Poland, PLN 180,000 net, is approx. €40,200; the price after delivery abroad is unknown.

Start: Sweden

  • The market exists: underwater treadmills are in use at a university hospital and in clinics.
  • 1,433 veterinary businesses (2023), approx. 355,500 horses (2016) and 34% of vets in chains, so a single decision may cover several sites.
  • In the EU: no customs duty, VAT is accounted for by the purchasing business.

Against:

  • There is competition, although it is not very visible: a Smartwalker is in use at one of the clinics; we have not established sellers or prices.
  • There are fewer veterinary businesses than in Austria and the Czech Republic; the law restricts treatments, and Swedish-language materials have to be created from scratch. Confidence: low to medium.

Alternative: Austria. Gain: a ready-made Petopo website in German, 2,111 veterinary businesses, approx. 150,000 horses. Loss: only veterinary surgeons may give treatment, so breeding kennels and stud farms do not provide therapy; German suppliers are close by.

Czech Republic: proximity and familiar hydrotherapy, but there is a local manufacturer offering rental, instalments and servicing within 48 h. England: the most competition and a country outside the EU.

Next step: confirmation from Petopo of delivery, installation and servicing in Sweden, then talks with a few clinics about price and requirements, before any money goes on advertising.

Questions for Petopo before market entry

  • Who in the chosen country supplies, installs, trains and services, how quickly and for how much?
  • Price with delivery in the country's currency, and what it covers.
  • CE documents and a manual in the country's language; permitted power supply and requirements for used water.
  • Is finance available for businesses abroad?
  • Can Petopo sell a water treadmill for horses abroad, and at what price?

Truth Checker

What I know

  • The manufacturer Vettronic operates in the Czech Republic, and at least two manufacturers in England.
  • In Austria, the examination and treatment of animals is reserved by law for veterinarians.
  • The UK Ministry of Defence bought a treadmill with servicing for £37,000 in 2019.

What I assume

  • That the lack of a visible seller in Sweden means easier entry.

What may be wrong

  • Sweden and Austria may have sellers that a quick search did not show.
  • Prices from different years and configurations are not directly comparable.
  • The data on horses and dogs come from different years and sources.

What to verify

  • Numbers of clinics in the three countries.
  • Regulations on treadmill hydrotherapy in Sweden.
  • Quotes from competitors.

What one input would improve

  • Information on which country Petopo has already had an enquiry from.
