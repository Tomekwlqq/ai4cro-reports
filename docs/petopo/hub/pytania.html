Questions for Petopo

Questions with a named recipient. First come those without which neither the page nor the ads can launch.

Block the launch

1. To: Petopo owner

Are we launching in Sweden (alternative: Austria), and who will deliver, install, train and service there, how quickly and at what cost?

Blocks: slogans, page, ads
2. To: Petopo owner

Have there already been any enquiries or contacts from any of these countries?

Blocks: choice of market
3. To: Petopo sales

Price including delivery in the country’s currency, and exactly what it covers.

Blocks: offer and page
4. To: Petopo technical team and manufacturer

CE documents and a manual in the country’s language; whether 230 V / 16 A is sufficient, what electrical protection is needed and how to drain used water containing animal hair.

Blocks: sales abroad
5. To: Petopo owner

Consent to publish photos showing people and to use the Fire Service’s name and photos abroad.

Blocks: page and ads
6. To: Petopo technical team

Can the therapist work in the water-filled chamber in the basic version, without the paid seat and platform? Can this be filmed?

Blocks: idea K2
7. To: Petopo owner and manufacturer

May Petopo sell a water treadmill for horses abroad? Under which brand, at what price and with which photos?

Blocks: ideas J1 and J2

To be clarified before the offer

8. To: Petopo technical team

Minimum floor area: the website states approx. 10 m², the playbook a 4.5 × 3.7 m room. Which figure applies?

Blocks: page content
9. To: Petopo technical team

The dog’s maximum weight and recommended height, unit dimensions, water temperature.

Blocks: page content and offer
10. To: Petopo service team

Warranty, inspections, response time and how long servicing is included in the price.

Blocks: idea K3
11. To: Petopo technical team

Heat pump: part of the system or a paid option? Do the low running costs apply to the version without it?

Blocks: price list
12. To: Petopo sales

Timing: 5 weeks for order fulfilment and 7 days to commissioning – how do these fit together?

Blocks: delivery time promises
13. To: Petopo sales

The financing example in the presentation does not match the price in the same presentation. What amount and how many months are the instalments based on? Is financing available for businesses abroad?

Blocks: calculation for the clinic
14. To: Petopo sales

Where does the per-session rate used in the presentation come from, and how many sessions does a facility actually run: 8–10 per day or 5–15 per week?

Blocks: calculation for the clinic
15. To: Petopo sales

Have there been any enquiries from breeders, and is there a cheaper configuration?

Blocks: idea H1
16. To: Petopo owner

Which brand should appear on the materials: Petopo or TechnoPet?

Blocks: page and ads
