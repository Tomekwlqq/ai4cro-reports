Veterinary clinics

K1. A new service at the clinic

alternative

Clinic owner or director, makes the purchase decision (playbook: medical segment 5/5)

Add canine hydrotherapy to your clinic with a treadmill configured for your space

One unit covers rehabilitation, training and recovery, and is made to order for the space in your treatment room.

For the ad Bring canine hydrotherapy into your clinic and use one treadmill for both rehabilitation and training.

Nobody has confirmed delivery, installation, training or finance in Sweden, so they are not in the slogan.

Hypothesis: the clinic wants to add hydrotherapy for post-procedure patients, patients with degenerative conditions and overweight patients, and needs a closed list: what it buys, what it prepares, who provides training, how it finances the purchase.

Idea: The page walks through introducing the service: what is included in the price, preparing the premises, team training, finance. The session calculation comes further down, once the instalments are confirmed.

Photos: Photos of the device from the client's library (whole unit, panel). A shot in a veterinary surgery is missing.

earlier analyses The calculation for the clinic and finance visible high up on the page. The figures from that table are dropped because they were calculated from PLN 60–85 thousand.

Page, evidence, objections, gaps

Page sections:

  • What the purchase includes (scope for Sweden to be confirmed)
  • Which patients: post-procedure, with degenerative conditions, overweight, seniors
  • Preparing the premises and commissioning
  • Price in kronor and paid options (once confirmed)
  • Session calculation in kronor (once prices are confirmed)
  • Premises requirements and a downloadable sheet

Evidence:

  • materials from Petopo Included in the price: delivery, installation, staff training and local servicing. Indicative price approx. PLN 180,000 net, depending on configuration. sales reply template; 2026 price list
  • materials from Petopo The clinic does not need previous experience; implementation means delivery, training, commissioning and support. presentation, slide 5
  • the Petopo website Help with preparing communications and marketing materials for the new service; finance or leasing is matched by PHINANCE advisers. health.petopo.com, accessed 26.11
  • materials from Petopo The presentation assumes PLN 200 of revenue per session and shows 20–60 sessions a month (PLN 4,000–12,000 of revenue, excluding costs). presentation, slide 8
  • materials from Petopo Instalments from the presentation: PLN 4,003 and PLN 3,272 a month. At PLN 200 per session, revenue alone matches the instalment at about 21 or 17 sessions a month. Excluding costs; to be shown only once the instalments are confirmed. presentation, slide 7; calculation

Objections:

  • PLN 180,000 net is a lot. Show the price in kronor with scope and options once the client confirms it. Do not promise finance or a payback period.
  • The team doesn't know how to do this. In Poland, training is part of implementation; to be confirmed for Sweden.

What is missing:

  • Confirmed instalments: from what amount and over how many months
  • Running costs
  • A first clinic as a reference

K2. Working with the patient in the treadmill

Veterinarian and animal physiotherapist, give their opinion on the purchase and work on the device

This underwater treadmill lets a physiotherapist correct the dog’s gait by hand

The therapist can step into the chamber, give manual therapy and observe the patient’s responses as they happen.

For the ad Choosing an underwater treadmill for your clinic? With ours, a physiotherapist can step into the chamber and correct the dog’s gait by hand.

A shot of a person in the water chamber is missing. In Sweden, the law restricts who may treat animals; a physiotherapist needs additional training.

Hypothesis: a patient after an orthopaedic or neurological procedure is to continue working at the facility, and the specialist wants to guide the movement by hand.

Idea: Open the page with the way of working: entering the chamber, gait correction, speed range. A clinical argument instead of an emotional one.

Photos: There are hands by the dog from the open-door side. There is no shot of a person in the chamber filled with water.

earlier analyses A video of about 60 seconds showing operation: opening the door, the dog entering, starting the belt at low speed, draining the water.

Page, evidence, objections, gaps

Page sections:

  • What the specialist does in the chamber
  • Session parameters: speed, water level, temperature
  • Therapy options: incline, counter-current, seat, platform, crossbar
  • Water hygiene and cleaning
  • Material for the facility owner: price and scope
  • Premises requirements and a downloadable sheet

Evidence:

  • materials from Petopo A physiotherapist or carer can enter the treadmill, carry out manual therapy and correct the movement pattern. Playbook: one of the most important advantages. playbook, advantages; offer, slide 2
  • materials from Petopo Speed 0.2–12 km/h, dry and in-water mode. presentation for a group of facilities, slide 8
  • materials from Petopo Options: 4% incline (PLN 10,000), artificial river (PLN 8,000), crossbar (PLN 2,500), seat (PLN 600), platform (PLN 2,500). 2026 price list, net
  • earlier analysis 22.10 A low starting speed matters for post-procedure and neurological patients. The comparison with a cheaper import has no source, so it does not go on the page. market report 22–23.10

Objections:

  • Will I fit next to a large dog? We have no confirmed range for the dog's size and weight. Do not state it until the client replies.
  • Where is the evidence of effectiveness? The materials contain no studies or cases. Do not promise treatment results.

What is missing:

  • Photo and video of a specialist in the chamber with water
  • Is a paid seat or platform needed for this work
  • Dog weight and height range
  • A statement from a vet or physiotherapist

Owner or manager of a clinic or rehabilitation centre; variant for a group of facilities

A canine underwater treadmill built for a fully booked clinic

The treadmill is built from stainless steel, and your team controls the water and belt from one panel. Emptying the chamber and refilling it with water from the storage tank takes 4–5 minutes at most.

For the ad Back-to-back sessions? Emptying the chamber and refilling it with water from the storage tank takes 4–5 minutes at most.

The headline comes from the client’s Polish website. The warranty, servicing and 5-week lead time have not been confirmed for Sweden.

Dog breeding kennels

H1. Dog fitness in a breeding kennel

Owner of a professional breeding kennel who makes the decisions and does the work personally (playbook: segment 3/5)

With your own underwater treadmill, you can give the dogs in your breeding kennel regular exercise

The treadmill can be used dry or with water and supports preventive care, preparation for shows and work, and recovery in dogs of different ages.

For the ad Do you breed dogs? One underwater treadmill supports preventive care, conditioning and recovery.

The rules on water training for healthy dogs in Sweden have not been confirmed. The slogan is about fitness, not treatment.

Hypothesis: the breeding kennel has several dogs of different ages, being prepared for shows, work or breeding, and wants to give them regular exercise on site.

Idea: One unit for the whole breeding kennel: prevention, conditioning for exertion, recovery. No stories about bonding.

Photos: No photos from a breeding kennel. To start with, shots of a dog in the chamber from the client’s library.

earlier analyses Dog breeding kennels do not appear in the older analyses. Only the premises requirements sheet has been carried over.

Page, evidence, objections, gaps

Page sections:

  • Uses in a breeding kennel
  • Working with a dog that is in water for the first time
  • Operation, water and cleaning
  • Premises requirements and a downloadable sheet
  • Price in kronor and paid options (once confirmed)

Evidence:

  • materials from Petopo Goal in the breeding segment: the dogs’ development, fitness and welfare, and preparation for work, shows and breeding. playbook, segment map
  • materials from Petopo One unit supports prevention, motor conditioning and recovery in dogs of different ages. playbook, arguments for personas
  • materials from Petopo The handler can get into the treadmill, which helps the dog adapt to working in water (client’s claim). playbook, arguments for personas

Objections:

  • Photos from a breeding kennel

Truth Checker

What I know

  • Petopo’s materials only mention dogs.
  • The manufacturer TechnoHorse shows a water treadmill for horses in three versions on its website (accessed 26.11).
  • The health.petopo.com website is currently aimed at veterinary clinics.

What I assume

  • All buyer situations are hypotheses.
  • That Petopo may sell a treadmill for horses. No material confirms this.

What may be wrong

  • At a price of PLN 180,000, a dog breeding kennel may not be a realistic buyer.
  • For horses, a “stud farm” may buy less often than a training or rehabilitation centre.

What to verify

  • Horses, leasing, number of sessions, working in the chamber, warranty.
