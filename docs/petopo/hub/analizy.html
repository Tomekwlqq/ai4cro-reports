Content analyses already carried out

What we found in Petopo's materials, on the websites and in earlier studies. A summary, with the source given for each part. Figures and claims from the client's materials have not been independently checked.

Sales playbook (version 1.0, 15.07.2026)

Source: Petopo materials
  • Positioning: not an “underwater treadmill” but a system for working with dogs: from prevention and motor conditioning to rehabilitation and recovery.
  • The most important advantage according to the playbook: a specialist or the dog's carer can get inside with the dog, carry out manual therapy and correct its movement.
  • Segments and potential rating: medical 5/5; sport and training, uniformed services and care 4/5 each; dog breeding kennels 3/5.
  • The conversation should be about the results of the work and the development of the facility, not just the specifications.
  • Blanks: unit dimensions, dog's weight and height, warranty, service inspections, certificates. Case studies marked as still to be written.
  • The playbook says nothing about horses.

Sales presentation, offer and price list

Source: Petopo materials
  • Arguments for facilities: a new service, implementation with no previous experience, training, servicing, configuration to suit the practice.
  • Implementation in four steps: delivery, training, commissioning, support.
  • Specifications from the presentation for a group of facilities: speed 0.2–12 km/h, dry and in-water operation, stainless steel, raisable floor.
  • Premises requirements from the playbook: door at least 90 cm, room at least 4.5 × 3.7 m, 220 V / 16 A, running water and a drain.
  • To be clarified: the heat pump is part of the system in one place and a paid option in another; the financing example does not match the price in the same presentation.

Photos and videos

Source: client's library
  • There are shots of the unit, the panel, dogs in the chamber and work with the dog from the open-door side.
  • We found no shot of a person standing in the water next to the dog, and that is the most important advantage.
  • Many of the photos come from a shoot with the Fire Service and show people; consent to publish has not been confirmed.

Live page health.petopo.com (accessed 26.11)

Source: Petopo website health.petopo.com
  • Aimed at veterinary clinics; headline about a fully booked clinic schedule.
  • Promises a warranty, servicing, fulfilment in 5 weeks, help with marketing materials, leasing and rental with a purchase option.
  • Shows work with the Fire Service.
  • Gives a minimum floor area of approx. 10 m², while the playbook gives a room of 4.5 × 3.7 m (approx. 16.7 m²). To be agreed.

Manufacturer's websites

Source: technopet.eu and technohorse.eu (accessed 26.11) technopet.eu technohorse.eu
  • Technopet: underwater treadmills for dogs for veterinary facilities, physiotherapy practices and rehabilitation centres.
  • Technohorse: water treadmill for horses in Basic, Standard and Premium versions; touchscreen, training log, heart-rate sensor, 3 × 400 V power supply. No price given.
  • None of the materials shows whether Petopo is able to sell the water treadmill for horses.

The “Three directions” analysis of 22.10

Source: earlier study
  • Review of the page from a business customer's point of view: too few technical specifics before the enquiry button.
  • Proposed layout: outcome and financing high up, trust bar, dimensions table, operating video, calculator, form with version selection.
  • Quick steps: a PDF sheet of premises requirements and a short operating video.
  • Comparison of manufacturers of underwater/water treadmills for dogs and horses. The prices quoted there have no sources, so we do not repeat them.

Market report of 22–23.10

Source: earlier study
  • Feature comparison: minimum speed, cleaning under the belt, method of water level control, door width, safety, belt incline.
  • Main players in the comparison: Petopo / Technopet, FizjoPet, Vettronic, Hydro Physio and imports from Asia; for horses FizjoPet, Horse Gym, Activo-Med and CET.
  • Advantage identified in the report: the therapist can work inside the chamber; with some competitors this is possible in larger models, so it is not exclusive.
  • Competitor specifications from this report need to be checked at source before use in advertising.

What this means

Source: our conclusions
  • Communication to clinics, breeding kennels and horse yards: matter-of-fact, about the work, the space, operation and servicing.
  • Needed before publication: a shot of work in the chamber, dimensions and the dog weight range, warranty terms, CE documents.
  • Abroad, nothing in the Polish offer is certain until Petopo confirms it.
