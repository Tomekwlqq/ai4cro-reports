Two directions for the new sales page

First screens only; the full page follows the chosen direction. Drafts: delivery, installation, servicing, warranty and financing abroad are not confirmed.

Recommendation: direction A, with the room check from B as its second section

A opens with the strongest argument and lets the clinic price its own build at once. B's room check answers the next question, so it follows straight after.

What both do, and why

  • Phone and e-mail from Petopo's live website are visible without scrolling: clinical equipment makers keep contact in the header (Hydro Physio, HydroWorx, AlterG).
  • The net price from the 2026 price list is shown early, next to a way to ask for a quote (Vetverta shows price and leasing on the product; Hudson puts “Request a quote” by the product photo).
  • Leasing has its own button. No instalments are shown, because the figures in the client's materials do not agree.
  • A real video of a person inside the empty chamber replaces mood photography; there is no photo of a person in the water-filled chamber and none was generated.
  • No three icon columns: that pattern is what made the earlier draft generic.

Direction A: “Waterline”: the therapist inside, then build your treadmill

Open direction A and try it

Why

  • The headline rests on the playbook's main advantage: a therapist can work inside with the patient.
  • The configurator is on the first screen: base price plus 12 options with a live net total, and the selection goes into the enquiry e-mail (Technopet offers a configurator behind a button; here the tool is already open).
  • Options are grouped by whom they serve, starting with the therapist inside, so the price list reads as the same story.

Weaker point: the room check is not on this screen.

Direction B: “Floor plan”: will it fit your treatment room?

Open direction B and try it

Why

  • The first question of any clinic is space: the plan redraws as the visitor types room length, width and door width, and compares them with the stated minimum.
  • Water, drain and power requirements are ticked off in the same tool and sent with the enquiry (HydroWorx uses door width as a buying argument).
  • A tall headline and one strong colour field set it apart from the usual clinical template (Vettronic for large type on a single field of colour).

Weaker point: no configurator on this screen, only the base price.

Still missing before a full page

  • A photo or film of a therapist working in the water-filled chamber.
  • Warranty, certificates and full dimensions.
  • Confirmed delivery, installation, servicing and financing abroad.
  • A form that sends somewhere: for now the buttons open a prepared e-mail.
