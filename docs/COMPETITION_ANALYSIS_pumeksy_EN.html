Key Gaps — what competitors do well vs. what they miss

🔴 P0 — No FAQ at ANY competitor

None of the 7 analyzed products has an FAQ section on PDP. This is a HUGE content gap in a category that generates many questions ("wet or dry?", "how to clean?", "for sensitive skin?", "how often to use?"). GLOV can dominate GEO with its FAQ.

🔴 P0 — No competitor has clinical proof

The entire pumice category relies on consumer proof (reviews, stars). Zero clinical studies, zero dermatological certifications (except Scholl — brand trust, but no specific certificate on PDP). GLOV did the same in makeup removal — and no one has it there either.

🟡 P1 — No one communicates TCO / lifespan

None of the manufacturers states a declared lifespan in years or a cost-per-use calculation. Yet nano glass lasts 2-5+ years vs. stone = 2-6 months. GLOV has the chance to be the first to show true cost-per-use (as it did in makeup removal: "replaces 400 cotton pads").

🟡 P1 — No "upgrade from stone" narrative

None of the competitors directly addresses the cep_upgrade_z_kamienia path. This is the strongest CEP for nano — the user already has a stone pumice that crumbles/slips/smells, and is looking for something better. No PDP says "tired of crumbling stone? Switch to nano."

🟡 P1 — Complete absence of comparative content

No manufacturer compares their product to alternatives (stone vs nano, file vs electric, budget vs premium). This leaves the customer with the question: "what's the difference?" without an answer. GLOV can build a comparison table on PDP.

🔵 P2 — No one has a before/after section

No visual proof of effectiveness in before/after form at any competitor. Given that the effect is immediate and spectacular (calloused heel → smooth in 30 seconds), this is a massively underused persuasion tool.

🔵 P2 — No one targets specific CEPs in PDP content

CS analysis (11 CEP for L1, 8 CEP for L2 nano) vs competitor PDP content: 0 coverage. No PDP addresses cep_wstyd_spoleczny, cep_pekajace_piety, cep_higiena_narzedzia or cep_domowy_pedicure.

🔵 P2 — Packaging / design not differentiated

Most products look similar: white/black body + colored element. Only GLOV has a recognizable palette (pink/black), Deni Carte has Exclusive Gold. The rest are visual commodities. GLOV can go further with strong brand packaging.

Key Takeaways

Main conclusion: The category is completely underserved content-wise.
The entire pumice market (L1 + nano L2) operates on 0 content marketing. No PDP has FAQ, none has comparative narrative, none targets CEP, none has clinical proof. Customers buy pumice guided by star rating + price — zero decision support.
GLOV has an open highway: it can be the first brand in the category to build a truly citable PDP (FAQ, comparisons, TCO, CEP). In makeup removal, GLOV was also the content leader — in pumice the opportunity is even greater, as competition is weaker.
Threat from below: KillyS (4.94★, 175 reviews, PLN 25) and Deni Carte (4.95★, 221 reviews, PLN 17) are collecting many positive reviews in the budget segment. GLOV must clearly communicate WHY it is worth 2× more — otherwise users will choose the budget option with a similar rating.
