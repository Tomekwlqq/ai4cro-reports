01
Goal

What we're building and why

Build a French-language Ambassador Program recruitment LP for the Envie de Plus platform (P&G France). The program is a content-for-product exchange: selected ambassadors receive P&G products for free and are eligible for prizes, in return for creating authentic social media content (Instagram / TikTok) featuring those products.

Pre-selected Envie de Plus members receive a personalised email invitation with a link to this LP. The page captures applications and social profile links so the agency can review content quality and select final ambassadors. Post-launch, all campaign management is handled by the agency independently.

02
Assumptions

What we're taking as given

  • All French copy, headlines, and body text provided by the agency before development begins
  • The page is built within the existing Envie de Plus platform infrastructure — to be confirmed
    If otherwise, the page still needs to be an almost official P&G digital asset — design and production quality matching P&G brand standards.
  • Envie de Plus brand guidelines and design assets (logo, color system) shared at kickoff
  • Campaign imagery (KV, product photos, content inspiration examples) supplied by the agency per campaign
  • Form destination (backend / email / webhook) confirmed by agency before build
  • Prizes section content (images, descriptions, quantities) provided and updated by agency — no dev required
  • French legal text (Règlement) provided by agency's legal team — not in our scope to write
  • The agency performs UAT and content sign-off before go-live

04
Flow

End-to-end recruitment flow

1
Selection — agency side Agency selects target customers from Envie de Plus database based on campaign criteria.
2
Email invitation Each selected customer receives a personalised email with a unique link to the recruitment LP.
3
Landing on the LP Interested customer clicks the link and arrives on the Ambassador Recruitment page — the deliverable of this project.
4
Page consumption Customer scrolls top-to-bottom: KV → benefits → prizes → eligibility → procedure → form.
5
Application submission Customer completes the form with personal details and Instagram/TikTok profile links, accepts RGPD consent, submits.
6
Agency review & selection Agency reviews submitted profiles and content quality, selects ambassadors, proceeds with product dispatch.
